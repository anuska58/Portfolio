Research milestone

ASIS&T 2026

Co-authored research · Model lineage

Understanding Model Reuse Patterns at Scale on Hugging Face

Across 341,070 text-generation models, we found that 97.8% of multi-parent reuse stayed within the same model family and 89.3% of derivatives remained within two generations of their origin.

My contribution · Led formal analysis, methodology, and data curation; co-wrote the original draft.
